The Illusion
of Escape.

Greenacre Park appears to offer an escape from the noise and intensity of Midtown. But how much of that tranquility is carefully constructed?

These two interactive prototypes explore how physical design, sensory conditions, and human behavior come together to produce the atmosphere of an urban park.
